Design Changes

I made multiple changes to make the website more professional and enticing. I gave the AI inspiration from Adidas and Abercrombie to understand the vibe I was going for. I added accent colors for buttons, which helps customers quickly identify how to buy things. I also added stock photos to help the site look more intruiging. I also created a full list of color, text, and other formatting rules - the full guidelines for the design changes are in `design rules.md` in the project root. Other changes included adding a header and footer, adding category tiles to shop by type of item, polished the product card pages, and made sure the theme and formatting was consistent throughout the site.
